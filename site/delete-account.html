Your account

Delete your Dhun account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app, at any time. It takes effect at once and signs you out on every phone.

In the app

  1. Open Dhun and go to the Me tab.
  2. Tap Account and security.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. If you sign in with an email address, enter your password.

If you can't open the app

Email support@dhunlive.com with the subject "Delete my account". Write from the email address on your account, or include the phone number you signed up with. We will send a code to that number or address to confirm the account is yours, then delete it within 7 days.

Before you delete Coins and gems you have not spent, and points you have not withdrawn, end with your account and cannot be recovered. If you are a host, withdraw your points first.

What happens to your data

Deleted straight away

  • Your phone number and email address
  • Your password
  • Your name, photo, bio, gender and date of birth
  • The languages you chose
  • Your devices and notification settings
  • Every signed-in session

Kept, without your name

  • A record of coin purchases, gifts and payouts, with no name, number or email attached
  • Messages and gifts you sent stay where they are, but no longer show your name

Indian tax and accounting law requires us to keep financial records. We keep them only as long as the law requires, currently up to eight years, and use them only for accounts, tax and resolving disputes.

Coming back

Once deleted, an account cannot be restored. You can sign up again with the same phone number, as a new account.
