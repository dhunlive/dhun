How a room works

Walk in, take a seat, join the song.

  1. 01

    Pick a room

    Browse what is live right now: singing, chatting, making friends, gaming. Every room shows its language.

  2. 02

    Take a seat

    Party rooms have up to 20 seats. Take a free one to talk or sing, or just listen and chat from the audience.

  3. 03

    Cheer with a gift

    From a rose to a rocket, a gift is how you thank a host. Hosts earn from every gift they receive.

Safety

A good room needs good rules.

No games of chance. Ever.

Dhun has no lucky draws, spins, mystery boxes or betting. A gift is a gift: you always know exactly what you are sending, and to whom.

Adults only

Dhun is for people aged 18 and over. Hosts confirm who they are with PAN and a face check before they can be paid.

Report or block anyone

From any room or profile, in two taps. A person reviews every report, and hosts can remove anyone from their room.

Limits you set

Set a daily or monthly spending limit, or pause spending for 30 days, 90 days or for good. Dhun enforces it, not just reminds you.

Your location stays yours

Dhun never asks where you are and never shows anyone how far away you live.
